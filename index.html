Privacy Policy – Satyo - Indian Card Game

Effective date: 6 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app “Satyo - Indian Card Game”

This policy explains what information is handled when you use Satyo - Indian Card Game (the “App”, shown on your device as “Satyo”), published by Aestivio Design (“we”, “us”). If you have a question, contact us at aestivio.design@gmail.com.

In short
  • You do not create an account, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not run our own servers, analytics or crash-reporting tools. Your game progress stays on your device.
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob and the ad networks AdMob works with in this App, AppLovin and Unity Ads. These companies collect device and advertising data, as described below.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy and ad choices.

1. Information stored only on your device

To make the game work, the App saves the following on your phone only. It is never sent to us:

  • Game settings (sound, music, vibration, language, difficulty).
  • Your virtual coin balance and the table and card-back themes you have unlocked.
  • Simple counters and dates used to space out ads, rating requests and update reminders (for example, rounds played since the last ad and the time of the last ad).

Android backup is turned off for the App, so this data is not copied to your Google account. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android settings.

2. Advertising

The App is free and is supported by ads. We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob can also request ads from these mediation partners, whose SDKs are included in the App:

  • AppLovin: applovin.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Google: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Personalised and non-personalised ads

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you open the App, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform asks for your choices about personalised ads and storing information on your device. If you do not agree, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads, which are based on general context (such as the app you are using and your approximate location) rather than your interests.
  • Some US states: where state privacy laws give you the right to opt out of personalised advertising, the same consent tool lets you opt out.
  • Change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy and ad choices (shown where required by law).
  • On any Android device you can reset your advertising ID, or delete it on newer Android versions, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

Rewarded ads and interstitial ads

  • Rewarded ads are optional. After some rounds, the App may offer you a short ad to watch. If the ad network confirms that you finished it, you receive virtual coins. Coins can only be used to unlock cosmetic table themes inside the App. They have no cash value, cannot be bought, sold or transferred, and are never used for gambling or betting.
  • Interstitial ads (full-screen ads) may be shown when you leave a round or match result screen, never during a round. They are shown only to players who have already unlocked every theme, and at most once every 3 rounds and 2 minutes.

3. Google Play features

The App uses Google Play’s in-app update feature (to tell you when a new version is available) and in-app review feature (to let you rate the App without leaving it). These are provided by Google Play under Google’s Privacy Policy. A review you submit is published on Google Play like any other review; we see it there, together with the public name Google shows with it. We receive no other information from these features.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of our own (no Firebase, no Crashlytics).
  • We do not sell your personal information. The ad partners named above may treat sharing of advertising data as “sharing” for personalised advertising under some US state laws. You can opt out as described in section 2.
  • The App contains no real-money gambling, purchases with real money, or chat with other users.

5. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App and personal information was collected, contact us and we will help, including by contacting the ad partners.

6. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR and similar laws)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, India or another place with data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete your personal data;
  • restrict or object to its processing;
  • data portability;
  • withdraw consent at any time (in Settings → Privacy and ad choices), without affecting processing that took place before;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal bases: personalised advertising, and storing or reading information on your device where the law requires it, are based on your consent, given in the consent form. Where the law allows, showing non-personalised ads, preventing fraud and measuring ads are based on legitimate interests (keeping a free App available and secure).

Because we hold no personal data on our own systems, requests about advertising data are best sent directly to Google, AppLovin or Unity using the links above. You can also write to us and we will help you.

7. Data deletion

  • Game data: uninstall the App, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Satyo → Storage → Clear data. This permanently deletes your settings, coins and unlocked themes.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and use the deletion tools of Google, AppLovin and Unity linked above.
  • Any other request: email aestivio.design@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

8. Security and retention

Data stored by the App stays in the App’s private storage on your device and is kept until you delete it as described above. The ad SDKs send data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and keep it for the periods stated in their own privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a feature or an ad partner. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We may also mention important changes in the App’s Google Play release notes.
